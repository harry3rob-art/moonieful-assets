What happens between now and the end of your call

  1. Before your call

    I read your page first

    The way a stranger would

    Before your call starts, I’ve already looked at your website the way a stranger would, so your 15 minutes go to answers instead of background questions.

  2. Whenever you like

    Reply with your one question

    One sentence is plenty

    Reply to your confirmation email with the one thing you most want a clear answer on. Your pricing page, the line at the top of your homepage, why enquiries went quiet. One sentence is plenty, and your question gets answered first on the call.

  3. On the day

    Come as you are

    Nothing to prepare

    There’s nothing to prepare and nothing to tidy up before we talk. Your fog is the point, and I can only clear what I can see.
