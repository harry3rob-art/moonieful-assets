Questions you might have

What if you look at my page and there's nothing wrong with it?

Then I tell you that, and it's a good call either way. Some owners leave a free call with nothing more to fix, and that confirmation is free too. Not every business has Brand Fog. Some just have a stranger who hasn't found them yet.

Are you going to tell me my business is the problem?

No. If a stranger loses the thread on your page, that's almost never because your work is bad. It's usually because your page explains things the way you'd explain them to someone who already trusts you, and a stranger showing up cold doesn't share that trust yet. That's a message problem, and it isn't a reflection on you. It's the one thing a MoonieView is built to find.

I've had a designer tell me to start over before. Is a MoonieView the same thing?

No, and this is exactly why I read before I redesign anything. A rebrand costs four figures and takes months, and a new logo can't rescue a sentence that doesn't explain what you sell. A MoonieView tells you whether your message is what's actually in the way, before you spend that money finding out. Most of the time, it is, and the fix is smaller than a rebrand.

Do I have to show you anything I'm embarrassed by?

No. I'm looking at what's already public: your website, the way you already talk about your business. Nothing behind the scenes, nothing you haven't already put out into the world.
