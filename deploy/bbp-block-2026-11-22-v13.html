You've got the business idea, so why is the first step so hard to pick?

You've got your idea, and maybe more than an idea. Maybe you've already filed your LLC, bought your domain and sketched a logo on the back of a receipt. But every time you sit down to move forward, the same list stares back at you. Do you need a website first? A business plan? Funding? An offer? A price?

So you open another tab. Then another. Somebody on Instagram says you need a brand before anything else, and somebody on YouTube says you need customers before you spend a dime, and both sound right.

Here's what Courtney, Miki and Dr. Rochelle see over and over with new founders: the idea is good, and the founder is ready to work. What's missing is the order.

What happens when your business pieces go in the wrong order

Most new founders run out of starting money long before they run out of ideas, because the money went to the right things at the wrong time:

  • A logo, paid for before the offer was clear, that gets redone six months later.
  • A full website built before anyone knew what the business sells or who buys it.
  • A loan application sent before the founder knew which records and credit factors a lender looks at first.

None of those purchases is a bad one. Each just came too early.

So what would change if you knew the order before you spent another dollar?

Questions founders ask us

Do I need an LLC already?

No. Build. Brand. Prepare. works for idea-stage and early-stage founders, and Courtney's phase helps you work out what your business needs to get started.

Do I need revenue?

No. Dr. Rochelle's phase doesn't require existing revenue.

Do I need business credit?

No. Dr. Rochelle can focus on your personal credit readiness, your resources, how much funding you'd need, your entity status and what lenders will expect later.

Will Build. Brand. Prepare. get me funded?

No one can promise that. Endura gives you funding-readiness education and guidance, and lenders and other funding sources make their own decisions.

Do I get a full website or a logo?

No. Your Brand phase includes one focused sales page, built around your roadmap, plus your messaging, founder story and pitches. A full website or new logo can be added as separate work.

How many revisions do I get?

One consolidated round of revisions on your Brand work. You send one list of changes within five business days of delivery, and Miki makes them.

How long does your whole project take?

About two weeks from your first appointment, as long as scheduling, payment, intake and your feedback stay on track. Two weeks is our target, not a guarantee.

What happens first?

Your free 15-minute fit call. If the package fits, you move into your agreement, payment and onboarding.

Can I buy just one phase?

Build. Brand. Prepare. follows all three phases in order. Courtney, Miki and Dr. Rochelle each offer standalone services separately.

What if I change my mind?

You can cancel in writing before your 90-minute intensive with Courtney. If we get your cancellation at least 48 hours before that session, you get $2,747 back and we keep a $250 administrative fee. Inside 48 hours, the package isn't refundable unless the law says otherwise. Your agreement spells out every detail before you sign.

Who sees my financial information?

Only Endura. Your detailed financial records stay with Dr. Rochelle's team unless you give permission to share them.
